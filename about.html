About mGeek

What is mGeek?

mGeek is a technology consulting and engineering company that provides custom software development, web development, research software, simulation, AI integration, server infrastructure, and technical consulting to clients worldwide.

Our Mission

To make high-quality technical expertise accessible to people and organizations that need practical, well-engineered solutions.

Our Approach

Solutions are designed around the client's actual requirements rather than forcing a predefined product. We combine academic research experience with practical software engineering to deliver results that work.

Clients communicate directly with a technical team rather than being separated from implementation by layers of sales personnel.

Founder

Dr. Prateek Raj Gautam

mGeek was founded by Dr. Prateek Raj Gautam, a technology professional with experience spanning software engineering, research, web development, infrastructure and technical consulting.
